Apply to the workshop

Spatial and spatio-temporal modelling with inlabru, taught by Prof. Janine Illian, with seminars on spatial disaggregation and from the National Bureau of Statistics, Tanzania. Dodoma, 15–18 September 2026.

Before you begin — costs. There is no fee to attend, but we are not able to fund any participant's travel, accommodation or subsistence. Participants must cover these themselves or through their institution. We have no bursaries and no partial support, and this will not change. Please only apply if you can realistically get to Dodoma for 15–18 September.
What to expect. About 15 minutes in total: a few questions about your background, a short timed knowledge check, and three brief written answers. You do not need any prior knowledge of INLA or inlabru — those are what we will teach. Applications close Friday 28 August 2026.
